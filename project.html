Project Overview

The project focuses on theoretical and algorithmic analysis for quantum sequential decision-making. It has two main goals:

  1. Derive lower and upper complexity bounds for approximately optimal quantum decision-making algorithms.
  2. Propose quantum algorithms that approach (or achieve) optimal complexity.
